# Collapsible festival days

Festival schedule days become independent native details disclosures, initially expanded. Their existing date headings are the toggles, with the native disclosure marker and existing pink title styling. An undated group uses “Date unknown”. Days keep their current order. Each day's artists render in the exact reverse of the existing sorted order, without mutating the catalog or changing stored schedules. Artist links, attendance checkboxes, and nested song disclosures continue to work independently.

Verify collapsing/reopening a day, another day remaining expanded, reversed artist order, and nested song disclosure behavior in the browser. Run lint and build, then commit and deploy through GitHub.
